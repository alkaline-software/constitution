# Changelog

## v0.1.0

1. **The window.** The frame, a sidebar that slides, and the content on a card, in light and dark and seven accents.
2. **The settings.** A window of their own: Appearance, General and About, with updates under the title card.
3. **Eight languages.** English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German and Spanish, following the system's.
